Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email support@tripacepro.com. We usually reply within two working days. For a bug, it helps to include your phone model and the app version shown at the bottom of the Today screen.

Which devices does Tripace Pro run on?

Android phones running Android 10 or newer. Sending workouts to a watch works with Garmin watches that accept structured workouts, such as the Forerunner range.

How do I put a workout on my Garmin watch?

Plug the watch into your phone with its Garmin charging cable (a USB-C version, or the USB-A one with a USB-C adapter), open the workout in Tripace Pro and tap Send to Garmin watch. Allow access when Android asks. Unplug the watch and the workout appears under Training → Workouts. A direct Garmin Connect link, with no cable, is in development.

How do workouts from my watch get back into my plan?

Turn on Strava in Garmin Connect (Settings → Connected Apps → Strava), then connect Strava in Tripace Pro's Settings and switch on "Workouts from your Garmin". When the app opens, it marks the planned session of the same sport and day done.

My GPS pace looks wrong

Pace settles after the first few seconds of running. Keep location set to precise, start the workout outdoors with a clear view of the sky, and turn off battery saver for Tripace Pro (Android Settings → Apps → Tripace Pro → Battery → Unrestricted).

How do I move or skip a session?

Tap the session, then pick another day under "Move to another day this week", or tap Skip.

How do I delete my data?

Everything is stored on your phone. Uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android Settings → Apps → Tripace Pro → Storage. See the privacy policy for data shared with Strava or Garmin.
