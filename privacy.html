Privacy policy

Effective 20 November 2026

Tripace Pro is a training app made by Westover Engineering LLC, a Utah company ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data Tripace Pro uses, where it goes and how to delete it.

In short: Tripace Pro has no user accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your plan, workouts, routes and heart rate stay on your phone. Data leaves the phone only for features you turn on: Strava, Garmin, sharing a file, the optional AI coach, and map tiles. We never sell your data.

What Tripace Pro stores on your phone

  • Your race, training days, fitness numbers (paces, heart-rate zones, power) and training plan.
  • Workouts you log: times, distances, effort, notes and personal bests.
  • For outdoor workouts recorded with GPS: the route (location points with time and altitude) and heart rate, if a monitor is connected.
  • Settings, including any keys you enter for Strava or an AI provider. These stay in the app's private storage.

We do not receive any of this, except as described under Garmin Connect below.

Location

Tripace Pro uses precise location only while you are recording an outdoor run or ride. It measures distance and pace, gives voice cues and draws your route. While a workout is recording, Android shows a notification. Tripace Pro does not use location at any other time and does not ask for background location.

Bluetooth

Tripace Pro uses Bluetooth to connect to a heart-rate monitor or smart trainer that you choose. It does not use Bluetooth to work out where you are.

Strava (optional)

If you connect Strava and send a workout, Tripace Pro uploads its route, times, elevation, heart rate, title and description directly from your phone to Strava. If you allow it, Tripace Pro also reads your recent Strava activities (sport, date, name, time and distance) to mark matching planned workouts done; what it reads stays on your phone. You can disconnect in Tripace Pro's Settings at any time, which also revokes Tripace Pro's access. Strava's privacy policy applies to data on Strava.

Garmin

Watch file over USB (available now). Sending a workout to your Garmin watch copies a workout file to the watch over the USB cable plugged into your phone. Nothing goes over the internet. If you choose to share the file instead, it goes only to the app or person you pick.

Garmin Connect link (in development). We are building an optional connection to Garmin Connect. When it is available and you turn it on:

  • You sign in on Garmin's own page and choose what to share. We never see your Garmin password.
  • Garmin gives us an access token for your account. We store it encrypted on our service and use it only to deliver the features below.
  • Workouts to Garmin: we send the sessions in your Tripace Pro plan (name, sport, steps, durations or distances, and targets) to your Garmin Connect calendar so they sync to your watch.
  • Activities from Garmin: Garmin sends us the activities you record, which can include sport, start time, duration, distance, pace or speed, heart rate, power, cadence, elevation and GPS route. We pass them to your phone so Tripace Pro can mark sessions done and update your forecast. We keep a copy only until your phone collects it, and never longer than 30 days.
  • We use Garmin data only to provide Tripace Pro's features to you. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone else, except service providers that host our service under confidentiality obligations, or where the law requires it.
  • You can disconnect in Tripace Pro's Settings or in Garmin Connect at any time. When you do, or when Garmin tells us you have removed access, we delete your token and any Garmin data we still hold within 7 days.

Our service runs on Cloudflare's infrastructure in the United States. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest.

Other ways data leaves your phone

  • Export GPX (optional). Exporting a workout creates a GPX file and opens Android's share menu. The file goes only to the app or person you pick.
  • AI coach (optional). If you add your own AI provider's address and key in Settings, your questions are sent to that provider, with a summary of your plan and recent workouts so it can answer. The provider's privacy policy applies. Nothing is sent unless you set this up.
  • Map tiles. To show a route on a map, the app downloads map images from OpenStreetMap, which sees your device's IP address and the area viewed. OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy.
  • App updates and page assets. When it opens, the app checks for updates on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts and the map library from cdnjs (Cloudflare). These services see your device's IP address, as with any website. No personal data is sent to them.
  • Android backup. If Android backup is on, Android may include Tripace Pro's data in your device backup to your Google account, following your phone's settings.

This website

tripacepro.com is hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and uses no analytics. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security, under GitHub's privacy statement.

Deleting your data

To delete everything on your phone, uninstall Tripace Pro or clear its storage in Android Settings → Apps → Tripace Pro → Storage. To delete single workouts, remove them in the app. To remove Garmin data we hold, disconnect Garmin, or email us and we will delete it within 7 days. Data already sent to Strava, Garmin or an AI provider is managed by that service.

Children

Tripace Pro is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the new version here with a new effective date. If a change affects data we already hold, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
