Tripace privacy policy

Effective 17 November 2026

In short: Tripace has no accounts, no Tripace server, no ads and no analytics. Your plan, workouts, routes and heart rate stay on your phone. Data leaves the phone only for features you turn on or use: Strava, sharing a GPX file, the optional AI coach, and map tiles.

What Tripace stores on your phone

  • Your race, training days, fitness numbers (paces, heart-rate zones, power) and training plan.
  • Workouts you log: times, distances, effort, notes and personal bests.
  • For outdoor workouts recorded with GPS: the route (location points with time and altitude) and heart rate, if a monitor is connected.
  • Settings, including any Strava or AI-provider keys you enter. These stay in the app's private storage.

The developer of Tripace never receives any of this.

Location

Tripace uses precise location only while you are recording an outdoor run or ride. It measures distance and pace, gives voice cues and draws your route. While a workout is recording, Android shows a notification. Tripace does not use location at any other time and does not ask for background location.

Bluetooth

Tripace uses Bluetooth to connect to a heart-rate monitor or smart trainer that you choose. It does not use Bluetooth to work out where you are.

When data leaves your phone

  • Strava (optional). If you connect Strava and send a workout, Tripace uploads its route, times, elevation, heart rate, title and description directly to Strava, using a Strava API application you create. Strava's privacy policy then applies. You can disconnect in Settings at any time, which also revokes Tripace's access.
  • Export GPX (optional). Exporting a workout creates a GPX file and opens Android's share menu. The file goes only to the app or person you pick.
  • AI coach (optional). If you add your own AI provider's address and key in Settings, your questions are sent to that provider, together with a summary of your plan and recent workouts so it can answer. The provider's own privacy policy applies. Nothing is sent unless you set this up.
  • Map tiles. To show a route on a street map, the app downloads map images from OpenStreetMap. This shows OpenStreetMap your device's IP address and the area being viewed. OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy.
  • App updates and page assets. Each time it opens, the app checks for updates on GitHub Pages. It also loads fonts from Google Fonts and the map library from cdnjs (Cloudflare). These services see your device's IP address, as with any website. No personal data is sent to them.
  • Android backup. If Android backup is on for your phone, Android may include Tripace's data in your device backup to your Google account. This follows your phone's backup settings.

Tripace does not sell data, show ads, or use advertising or analytics tools.

Deleting your data

To delete everything, uninstall Tripace or clear its storage in Android Settings → Apps → Tripace → Storage. To delete single workouts, remove them in the app. Data already sent to Strava or an AI provider is managed by that service.

Children

Tripace is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
